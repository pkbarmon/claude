## Why the page needs copy
Today the page is an H1, one sentence and a form. The recommendation only renders after someone submits, so Google and AI answer engines see almost no text and have nothing to rank, quote or cite. The copy below adds an indexable layer around the tool without changing how the tool works.

**Search intent this page should own:** "which Maui luau is right for me" decision and quiz searches, plus long-tail "best luau in Maui for [families / couples / seniors / budget]". It shouldn't compete with `/best-maui-luaus` (rankings) or the blog comparisons. It links to them instead.

### H2: How the Maui Luau Finder works

1. **Tell us about your group.** Enter guest count, kids, mobility needs and the occasion.
2. **Set your budget and base.** Add your per-adult budget and where you're staying (West Maui, Wailea or Kīhei). Drive time matters: West Maui to Wailea is about an hour each way at sunset.
3. **Get a match with reasons.** You get one best-fit luau, two alternatives and practical tips, each tied to your answers. Every pick links to its booking page with the current price.

### H2: Quick answers: the best Maui luau by trip type

Each answer is short and self-contained so AI engines can quote it on its own.

**What is the best Maui luau for families with kids?**
Huakaʻi Lūʻau in Kāʻanapali is the best fit for most families. Children's tickets start at $81.31, infants are free, the buffet has gluten-free options, and the venue is fully ADA accessible. Myths of Maui is the alternative if you want a more traditional show.

**What is the best Maui luau for couples and honeymoons?**
The Lūʻau at Grand Wailea with Aliʻi Premium seating ($360 per adult) is the top pick for couples. You get a front-row private table, a welcome cocktail and premium spirits with a three-course dinner on Wailea Beach. On the West Side, Huakaʻi VIP ($237.14) is the lower-cost romantic option.

**What is the cheapest luau on Maui?**
The Sugar Beach Sunset Luau in Kīhei is the lowest-priced Maui luau at $100 per person. The ticket covers the oceanfront sunset show only. Food and drinks are bought on-site, and you bring your own beach chair or blanket.

**Which Maui luau is best for seniors or guests with limited mobility?**
Huakaʻi Lūʻau is the safest choice because its venue is fully ADA accessible. The Lūʻau at Grand Wailea is also a good fit thanks to seated table service. Sugar Beach is the hardest for limited mobility, since guests sit on the lawn in their own chairs.

**Which Maui luau is best for gluten-free or vegetarian diets?**
Huakaʻi Lūʻau always has gluten-free options on its buffet. At Sugar Beach every menu item is labeled, including a vegan vegetable pad thai and gluten-free shoyu chicken. At any luau, note dietary needs when you book, not at the door. See our [vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free luau guide](/blog/maui-luau-vegetarian-vegan-gluten-free).

**Which Maui luaus include an open bar?**
Huakaʻi, Myths of Maui and The Lūʻau at Grand Wailea all include drinks in the ticket. Sugar Beach is cash bar only. Full breakdown: [Maui luaus with an open bar](/blog/maui-luaus-with-open-bar).

**I'm staying in Wailea or Kīhei. Which luau should I book?**
Book The Lūʻau at Grand Wailea for a full-service evening, or the Sugar Beach Sunset Luau for a budget one. Both are within about 15 minutes, while the Kāʻanapali luaus are about an hour's drive each way. See [Wailea luaus compared](/blog/wailea-luaus-compared).

**I'm staying in Kāʻanapali or Lahaina. Which luau should I book?**
Choose Huakaʻi for an all-round oceanfront evening with an open bar, or Myths of Maui for Maui's longest-running traditional show. Both are on Kāʻanapali Beach, so you can usually walk. See [Kāʻanapali luaus compared](/blog/kaanapali-luaus-compared).

### H2: 4 things that decide which luau fits you

1. **Where you're staying.** A luau 15 minutes away beats a "better" one an hour away, especially after an open bar.
2. **What the ticket includes.** $100 at Sugar Beach and $203 at Huakaʻi aren't like-for-like: one includes dinner and drinks and the other doesn't.
3. **Seating tier.** VIP and premium tiers buy table position and extras, not a different show. Read the [Maui luau seating guide](/blog/maui-luau-seating-guide).
4. **Fees after checkout.** Grand Wailea's 24% service charge adds about $72 per standard ticket. See [how much a Maui luau really costs](/blog/how-much-does-a-maui-luau-cost).

### H2: Frequently asked questions

**Is the Maui Luau Finder free?**
Yes. It's free, needs no sign-up and doesn't ask for an email address. You only pay if you choose to book a luau.

**How does the luau finder choose a luau?**
It compares your answers (group size, kids, mobility, occasion, budget, hotel area and must-haves) with each luau's price, location, length, what's included and important notes. It then returns one best match, two alternatives and tips, and only recommends luaus listed on this site.

**Are the prices in the recommendations accurate?**
The finder uses the starting prices from each luau's booking page on this site. The final price, including tax and any venue service charge, is confirmed on the booking page before you pay.

**What is the best luau in Maui?**
It depends on your trip. Huakaʻi is the best all-rounder for families on the West Side. The Lūʻau at Grand Wailea is best for luxury and couples. Sugar Beach is best on a budget. Myths of Maui is best for a traditional show with an imu ceremony.

**How far in advance should I book a Maui luau?**
Book 2–3 weeks ahead in peak season (mid-June through August and mid-December through March). VIP and front-row seats sell out first. See [how far in advance to book a Maui luau](/blog/how-far-in-advance-to-book-a-maui-luau).

**Can the finder plan a luau for a wedding or large group?**
Yes, for groups of up to 50 guests. Choose "Wedding or group event" as the occasion and the finder will weigh seating, budget and location for the group. For private events or buyouts, contact the venue directly.
