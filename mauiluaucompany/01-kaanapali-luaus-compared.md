# Kaanapali Luaus Compared: Which Ka'anapali Beach Luau Is Best?

> **Quick answer:** Ka'anapali has five resort luaus within about 2 miles of each other. **Huaka'i Luau** (Hilton Vacation Club Ka'anapali Beach) is the best all-rounder for an oceanfront setting. **Drums of the Pacific** (Hyatt Regency Maui) has the biggest stage production. **Maui Nui Luau** (Sheraton) is the only one staged beside the Black Rock cliff dive. **Wailele** (Westin Maui) sits in the middle of the beach walk. **Myths of Maui** (Royal Lahaina Resort) is the longest-running luau on Maui.

*Updated September 2026 · Reviewed by the Maui Luau Company editorial team*

*Disclosure: Huaka'i Lu'au is our own show. We have placed it on the same criteria as every other luau below, and we list where others beat it.*

Ka'anapali is West Maui's resort strip: three miles of beach between Pu'u Keka'a (Black Rock) and the Hyatt Regency, joined by the paved Ka'anapali Beach Walk. Each of the five Ka'anapali luaus is hosted by a resort, and all are open to the public. Your choice usually comes down to three questions: how big a show you want, whether the Black Rock sunset matters, and how far you want to walk after dinner.

For the neighborhood overview, see our [Ka'anapali luau location guide](/maui-luau-locations/kaanapali). This page is the side-by-side comparison.

## Which Ka'anapali luau should you book?

### Best overall: Huaka'i Luau

Huaka'i is set directly on the sand-side lawn of Hilton Vacation Club Ka'anapali Beach, so the ocean sits behind the stage for the whole show. It runs about 2.5 hours with a Hawaiian-inspired buffet, an open bar with mai tais, live island music, and a Polynesian revue. It has two points most Ka'anapali luaus don't advertise: the venue is **fully ADA accessible**, and there are **gluten-free options on the buffet** every night. In bad weather the show moves to the resort ballroom rather than being cancelled outright.

- **Price:** from $203.26 per adult (General Admission), $237.14 for VIP seating
- **Kids:** from $81.31, and infants are free
- **Choose it if:** you want an oceanfront luau with an open bar and fewer logistics.
- **Skip it if:** the biggest stage spectacle matters most to you. Drums of the Pacific is the larger production.

[See Huaka'i dates →](/activities/huakai-luau-huakai-luau-maui-general-admission)

### Biggest show: Drums of the Pacific (Hyatt Regency Maui)

Drums of the Pacific has run at the Hyatt Regency at the south end of Ka'anapali for more than three decades. The revue travels through Hawai'i, Tahiti, Samoa and Aotearoa (New Zealand) before the Samoan fire knife finale. At roughly 300 seats it is one of the larger Ka'anapali shows, which suits first-time luau guests and groups.

- **Choose it if:** you want the most theatrical production, or you're staying at the Hyatt.
- **Skip it if:** you want an intimate, quieter evening.

### Best Black Rock sunset: Maui Nui Luau (Sheraton Maui)

This is the only Ka'anapali luau staged beside **Pu'u Keka'a (Black Rock)**. Just before the show, a torch lighter climbs the rock at sunset and dives into the ocean, a nightly Sheraton tradition. The luau itself is shorter and simpler. The ticket includes a buffet and two drink tickets, with a cash bar after that.

- **Choose it if:** you want the cliff dive and the sunset photos.
- **Skip it if:** an unlimited open bar matters to you.
- *Also searched as:* Kaanapali Sunset Luau at Black Rock, Sheraton Kaanapali Luau.

### Most central: Wailele Polynesian Luau (Westin Maui)

The Westin sits mid-beach, a few minutes' walk from Whalers Village and the other big resorts. Wailele is a mid-sized show (roughly 275 seats) that follows the classic format: lei greeting, imu unveiling, buffet, and a fire knife finale.

- **Choose it if:** you're staying mid-beach and want to walk to dinner.

### Longest-running: Myths of Maui (Royal Lahaina Resort)

Myths of Maui has run since 1980 on the oceanfront lawn of the Royal Lahaina Resort at the north end of Ka'anapali. It keeps a traditional shape: imu ceremony, all-you-care-to-eat buffet, open bar, then a Polynesian revue that ends in fire. Kids 3 and under eat free.

- **Price:** from $208.94 per adult, $130.10 per child
- **Choose it if:** you want tradition and you're staying at the north end (Kāʻanapali Shores, Honokowai).

## Map: where each Ka'anapali luau sits (south → north)

1. **Hyatt Regency Maui**: Drums of the Pacific (south end, 200 Nohea Kai Dr)
2. **Westin Maui**: Wailele (2365 Ka'anapali Pkwy)
3. **Whalers Village** (shops and parking, mid-beach)
4. **Hilton Vacation Club Ka'anapali Beach**: Huaka'i
5. **Sheraton Maui**: Maui Nui Luau at Black Rock (2605 Ka'anapali Pkwy)
6. **Royal Lahaina Resort**: Myths of Maui (north end, 2780 Keka'a Dr)

The whole strip is roughly 2 miles by the beach walk. If you are staying in Kā'anapali, you can walk to any of the five, which avoids parking fees and makes the open bar easier. Driving from Kīhei or Wailea takes 50 to 70 minutes, depending on traffic at Maʻalaea and the Pali. See our [parking and drive-times guide](/blog/maui-luau-parking-and-drive-times).

## How to choose in 30 seconds

- **Staying in Ka'anapali?** Book the luau you can walk to. Location beats small differences between shows.
- **Want an open bar?** Huaka'i, Drums, Wailele, and Myths of Maui have one. Maui Nui gives two drink tickets.
- **Want the Black Rock cliff dive?** Maui Nui (Sheraton).
- **Need step-free access or gluten-free food?** Huaka'i publishes both.
- **Want the most authentic Hawaiian program, no fire knife?** None of these. Drive 10 minutes south to [Old Lahaina Luau](/luau-companies/old-lahaina-luau).
- **Traveling with kids under 4?** Myths of Maui (3 and under free) or Huaka'i (infants free).

## Tips for any Ka'anapali luau

- **Book 2–3 weeks out in peak season** (mid-June–August, mid-December–March). VIP and front-row seats go first. See [how far in advance to book](/blog/how-far-in-advance-to-book-a-maui-luau).
- **Pay for better seats only if the show matters more than dinner.** Our [seating guide](/blog/maui-luau-seating-guide) explains when VIP is worth it.
- **Sunset timing:** Ka'anapali faces west toward Lāna'i and Moloka'i. Luaus start between about 5:00 and 5:45 PM so the sun sets during dinner.
- **Bring a light layer.** Trade winds pick up on the beach after dark.
- **Rain:** Showers are brief on the dry West Maui side. See [do Maui luaus cancel for rain](/blog/do-maui-luaus-cancel-for-rain).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best luau in Ka'anapali?**
For most visitors, Huaka'i Luau is the best all-round Ka'anapali luau thanks to its oceanfront lawn, open bar, and gluten-free options. Drums of the Pacific at the Hyatt Regency is best for the biggest stage show, and Maui Nui at the Sheraton is best for the Black Rock sunset cliff dive.

**How many luaus are in Ka'anapali?**
Five resort luaus run in Ka'anapali in 2026: Huaka'i (Hilton Vacation Club), Drums of the Pacific (Hyatt Regency), Wailele (Westin), Maui Nui at Black Rock (Sheraton), and Myths of Maui (Royal Lahaina Resort).

**Is there a luau on Ka'anapali Beach?**
Yes. Huaka'i, Maui Nui, Wailele, and Myths of Maui are all staged on oceanfront lawns facing Ka'anapali Beach. Drums of the Pacific is on the Hyatt Regency's oceanfront grounds at the south end of the beach.

**Can you go to a Ka'anapali luau if you are not staying at the resort?**
Yes. All five Ka'anapali luaus are open to the public. Non-guests book tickets directly and either walk the Ka'anapali Beach Walk or park at the host resort. Some resorts validate parking for luau guests, so ask at check-in.

**What is the Ka'anapali Sunset Luau at Black Rock?**
It is the Sheraton Maui's luau, also sold as the Maui Nui Luau. It runs beside Pu'u Keka'a (Black Rock) and starts right after the resort's nightly sunset cliff-diving ceremony.

**How much is a luau in Ka'anapali?**
Adult general admission at Ka'anapali luaus runs from about $165 to $235 in 2026, before tax. VIP and front-row seating costs more, children are usually around half price, and some venues let infants in free.
