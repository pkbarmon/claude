## Rules for these links
1. **Link to complementary pages, not competing ones.** Both sites have luau articles. Sending "Maui luau" readers to hawaiian-activities.com's `/maui-luau-guide/` would push its page up against your own ranking pages. Link out for things mauiluaucompany.com doesn't cover: other activities, other islands, airport and transport, and luaus you don't sell.
2. **One or two contextual links per post,** placed in a sentence where the topic already comes up. Don't add sitewide footer or header links between the two sites. Google treats sitewide cross-links between sites with the same owner as a link scheme.
3. **Use descriptive, varied anchor text.** Never repeat the same exact-match anchor such as "Hawaii activities" across posts.
4. **Use normal links (no `nofollow`).** These are editorial links that help readers. Open them in the same tab and don't add UTM parameters.
