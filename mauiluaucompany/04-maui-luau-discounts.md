# Maui Luau Discounts in 2026: What Actually Works (and What Doesn't)

> **Quick answer:** Most "Maui luau promo codes" online are expired or fake. The discounts that actually work in 2026 are: **kamaʻāina rates** (Hawaiʻi ID required), **military rates** at some venues, **Costco Travel luau packages** (Drums of the Pacific), **reseller specials** of about $15–$30 off, **kids-free rules** (usually ages 3–5 and under), **off-season promo pricing**, and **choosing a lower-cost luau**, which saves the most, often $100+ per adult.

*Updated September 2026 · Reviewed by the Maui Luau Company editorial team*

Luau tickets are priced by the resort, and seats sell out in peak season, so operators rarely need to discount. What you'll mostly find is a small reseller markdown, a local or military rate, or a seasonal price drop. Here is what works, venue by venue.

## The 7 Maui luau discounts that actually work

### 1. Pick a lower-cost luau (saves $100–$260 per adult)

This is the biggest saving, and nobody puts it on a coupon site. Adult prices on Maui range from **$100 to $360**:

| Luau | Adult from | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| [Sugar Beach Sunset Luau](/activities/sugar-beach-sunset-luau-kihei), Kīhei | **$100** | Oceanfront sunset show. Food and drinks bought on-site ($8–$24 per item). Bring your own chair. |
| Aloha Wailea Luau (Wednesdays) | ~$160 | Hawaiian feast + open bar |
| Maui Nui Luau at Black Rock (Sheraton) | ~$165 | Buffet + 2 drink tickets |
| [Huakaʻi Lūʻau](/activities/huakai-luau-huakai-luau-maui-general-admission), Kāʻanapali | $203 | Buffet + open bar. **Infants free.** |
| [Myths of Maui](/activities/myths-of-maui-luau-at-the-royal-lahaina-resort-general-admission), Kāʻanapali | $209 | Buffet + open bar. **Kids 3 and under free.** |
| [The Lūʻau at Grand Wailea](/activities/standard-seating-at-the-luau-at-grand-wailea) | $300 + 24% service | 3-course dinner + open bar |

A family of four at Sugar Beach ($400, plus around $80–$100 for food) spends roughly **$600–$800 less** than at the Grand Wailea. For the full ranking, see [the cheapest Maui luaus](/blog/cheapest-maui-luaus).

*Disclosure: Huakaʻi Lūʻau is our own show.*

### 2. Kamaʻāina (Hawaiʻi resident) rates

Most Maui luaus offer a kamaʻāina rate to Hawaiʻi residents with a **valid Hawaiʻi driver's license or state ID**, checked at the door. Two things to know:
- The rate often applies **only to the resident**, not to visiting guests in the same booking.
- It's usually booked **by phone or at the box office**, not through online resellers.

### 3. Military discounts

Several luaus and authorized resellers offer military pricing with a valid military ID. Ask when booking directly. The rate isn't always shown online, and it usually doesn't combine with other offers.

### 4. Costco Travel luau packages

Costco Travel sells Maui vacation packages that include luau tickets, such as a **Hyatt Regency Maui package with two standard-seat tickets to Drums of the Pacific**. Read the fine print: these are often **booked automatically for the first available luau night after you arrive**, and luau nights vary by week. Costco doesn't usually sell standalone luau tickets, so "Old Lahaina Luau Costco" searches mostly lead nowhere.

### 5. Reseller specials ($15–$30 off)

Authorized Maui activity desks often list luaus slightly below the venue's rate. For example, one reseller listed Drums of the Pacific at $194.95 against a $220 regular price in 2026. Before booking, check that:
- the seller is an authorized reseller,
- the price includes tax and fees, and
- the cancellation terms are the same as booking direct.

### 6. Kids-free and child pricing

This is often the most valuable "discount" for families:
- **Myths of Maui:** kids 3 and under eat free. Children are $130.10.
- **Huakaʻi:** infants free. Children are $81.31.
- **Te Au Moana (Wailea Marriott) and Aloha Wailea:** kids 5 and under free.
- **Grand Wailea:** ages 3–12 are typically about half the adult price.

Book infants and toddlers as their own ticket type (often $0) so a seat is saved for them.

### 7. Off-season and promo pricing

Some resorts cut prices outside peak season. For example, Te Au Moana at the Wailea Beach Marriott lists a promotional adult rate of about **$225** (vs. about $286) for **August 16, 2026 through January 31, 2027**. The cheapest months to see a Maui luau are usually **late April–May and September–early November**. See [how far in advance to book](/blog/how-far-in-advance-to-book-a-maui-luau).

## Discounts that usually don't work

- **Promo codes from coupon sites.** Codes for Myths of Maui, Royal Lahaina Luau, Drums of the Pacific, Westin, Sheraton, Andaz, and Grand Wailea are mostly expired, limited to one reseller, or never existed. See our deep dive: [Myths of Maui discounts and promo codes](/blog/myths-of-maui-luau-discounts-and-promo-codes).
- **Groupon.** Deals appear occasionally for smaller luaus such as Aloha Wailea. They are rare for the big resort shows, and resold vouchers often have date limits.
- **Last-minute walk-up deals.** Maui luaus don't sell unsold seats cheaply at the door. In peak season they sell out. Waiting usually costs you your preferred seats, not money.
- **"Free luau" with a timeshare tour.** You pay with a 90–120 minute sales presentation, and the luau offered is usually a fixed night and a general seat.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are there promo codes for Maui luaus?**
Almost never. Maui luau operators rarely publish public promo codes. Most codes on coupon sites are expired or tied to a single reseller. Kamaʻāina rates, military rates, kids-free rules, and seasonal promo pricing are the discounts that actually work.

**Does the Royal Lahaina Luau (Myths of Maui) have a discount?**
There is no public promo code for Myths of Maui. The savings that work are its kids-3-and-under-free rule, child pricing of $130.10, and occasional reseller specials. Hawaiʻi residents should ask about a kamaʻāina rate.

**Does Costco sell Maui luau tickets?**
Costco Travel sells Maui vacation packages that include luau tickets, such as a Hyatt Regency Maui package with Drums of the Pacific. It generally doesn't sell standalone luau tickets.

**Is there a military discount for Maui luaus?**
Some Maui luaus and authorized resellers offer military pricing with a valid ID. It's usually booked by phone and isn't always shown online.

**What is the cheapest luau on Maui?**
The Sugar Beach Sunset Luau in Kīhei is the lowest-priced Maui luau at $100 per person. Food and drinks are bought separately on-site.
