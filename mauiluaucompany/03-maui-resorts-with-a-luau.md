# Which Maui Resorts Have a Luau? A Hotel-by-Hotel Guide

> **Quick answer:** Eight Maui resorts host a luau on their own grounds in 2026. In Kāʻanapali: **Hyatt Regency** (Drums of the Pacific), **Westin** (Wailele), **Sheraton** (Maui Nui at Black Rock), **Hilton Vacation Club Kāʻanapali Beach** (Huakaʻi), and **Royal Lahaina** (Myths of Maui). In Wailea: **Grand Wailea**, the **Wailea Beach Marriott** (Te Au Moana), and **Andaz Maui** (Feast at Mōkapu). The Four Seasons, Fairmont Kea Lani, Montage, and Ritz-Carlton do not host a regular public luau.

*Updated September 2026 · Reviewed by the Maui Luau Company editorial team*

*Disclosure: Huakaʻi Lūʻau is our own show.*

Searching "[your hotel] luau" usually gets you a booking page with no clear answer. This page gives the answer for every major Maui resort: whether it hosts a luau, what the luau is called, and the nearest alternative if it doesn't. You do **not** need to stay at a resort to attend its luau. Every luau below is open to the public.

## Hotel by hotel

### Kāʻanapali

**Does the Hyatt Regency Maui have a luau?** Yes. *Drums of the Pacific* runs on the resort's oceanfront grounds at 200 Nohea Kai Drive. It's a large Polynesian revue with a buffet, open bar, imu ceremony, and fire knife finale.

**Does the Westin Maui have a luau?** Yes. The *Wailele Polynesian Luau* runs on the Westin's oceanfront lawn at 2365 Kāʻanapali Parkway, with a buffet, open bar, and a show of about 2 hours 15 minutes.

**Does the Sheraton Maui have a luau?** Yes. The *Maui Nui Luau*, also sold as the Kāʻanapali Sunset Luau, runs beside Puʻu Kekaʻa (Black Rock) and starts right after the nightly sunset cliff dive.

**Does the Hilton on Maui have a luau?** Yes. *Huakaʻi Lūʻau* is hosted at **Hilton Vacation Club Kāʻanapali Beach**, oceanfront on Kāʻanapali Beach. It runs 2.5 hours, is fully ADA accessible, and has gluten-free buffet options. Hilton Honors guests in Wailea usually attend The Lūʻau at Grand Wailea, which is a Waldorf Astoria, part of the Hilton family.

**Does the Royal Lahaina Resort have a luau?** Yes. *Myths of Maui*, running since 1980, is Maui's longest-running luau. It's staged on the Royal Lahaina's oceanfront lawn at the north end of Kāʻanapali, despite the "Lahaina" in the resort name.

**Does the Kāʻanapali Beach Hotel have a luau?** The former Kāʻanapali Beach Hotel is now **Outrigger Kāʻanapali Beach Resort**. It's best known for free Hawaiian music and hula in its courtyard, not a nightly full luau. For a full luau, walk 5 minutes to the Westin or to Huakaʻi.

**Kāʻanapali Aliʻi, Marriott's Maui Ocean Club, Kāʻanapali Beach Club (Diamond):** No on-site luau. All are within walking distance of the Kāʻanapali luaus above. See [Kāʻanapali luaus compared](/blog/kaanapali-luaus-compared).

### Kapalua and Nāpili

**Does the Ritz-Carlton Maui, Kapalua have a luau?** No regular public luau. The nearest are Myths of Maui and the Sheraton's Maui Nui Luau, about 15–20 minutes' drive south.

**Montage Kapalua Bay / Nāpili Kai:** No regular public luau. Go to Kāʻanapali, 15–20 minutes south.

### Wailea and Mākena

**Does the Grand Wailea have a luau?** Yes. *The Lūʻau at Grand Wailea* runs 2 hours 45 minutes with a three-course family-style dinner and hosted open bar. It was named to USA Today 10Best's Best Lūʻau in Hawaiʻi list for 2025. Standard is $300 and Aliʻi Premium is $360, plus a 24% service charge and tax.

**Does the Wailea Beach Marriott have a luau?** Yes. *Te Au Moana* (Hawaiian for "the ocean tide") serves a three-course dinner at the table with an open bar and a fire knife finale.

**Does Andaz Maui have a luau?** Yes. *The Feast at Mōkapu* is a small, chef-driven luau on Mōkapu Beach with a three-course ʻohana-style menu.

**Does the Four Seasons Maui have a luau?** Not on property. The concierge books guests into a **Family Luau** about 5 minutes' drive away, with communal tables and a family-style coursed menu. Kids 5 and under are free. See [Wailea luaus compared](/blog/wailea-luaus-compared).

**Fairmont Kea Lani, Hotel Wailea, Wailea Beach Villas:** No on-site public luau. Guests go to Te Au Moana, Grand Wailea, or Feast at Mōkapu.

**Makena Beach & Golf Resort area:** No on-site luau. Wailea luaus are 5–10 minutes north.

### Kīhei

**Do any Kīhei hotels have a luau?** No major Kīhei hotel runs a nightly luau. The nearest option is the **[Sugar Beach Sunset Luau](/activities/sugar-beach-sunset-luau-kihei)** on the oceanfront lawn at Sugar Beach Events. It costs $100 per person, doors open at 4:30 PM, the show runs 5:00–7:00 PM, you bring your own chair or blanket, and food and drinks are sold on-site.

### Lahaina town

**Is there a luau in Lahaina town?** Yes. **[Old Lahaina Luau](/luau-companies/old-lahaina-luau)** at 1251 Front Street survived the August 2023 wildfire and reopened in March 2024. It is not attached to a hotel. See [is Feast at Lele open](/blog/is-feast-at-lele-open) for the full Lahaina picture.

## Should you book your own hotel's luau?

**Usually yes, if your hotel has one.** Walking back to your room after an open bar beats a 60-minute night drive, and you skip resort parking fees (often $30–$55 for non-guests). Book a different luau when:

- **You want a specific feature** your hotel's luau doesn't have, such as the Black Rock cliff dive (Sheraton), a plated dinner (Wailea), or no fire knife and more traditional hula ([Old Lahaina Luau](/luau-companies/old-lahaina-luau)).
- **Your hotel's luau is sold out.** In Kāʻanapali, the next resort's luau is a short walk away.
- **The price gap is large.** For example, Wailea guests can save about $150 per adult at Aloha Wailea or Sugar Beach.

## Do hotel guests get a discount on the resort's luau?

Sometimes, but not reliably. Some resorts offer in-house rates or bundle luau tickets with room packages. Ask the concierge and compare with the public rate. Listings on this site show the full price with no hidden booking fee. See [Maui luau discounts that actually work](/blog/maui-luau-discounts).

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to stay at the resort to attend its luau?**
No. Every resort luau on Maui is open to the public. Non-guests book tickets the same way and park at the resort, sometimes with luau validation.

**Which Maui hotel has the best luau?**
It depends on what you want. The Grand Wailea is the award winner for luxury. The Hyatt Regency's Drums of the Pacific has the biggest production. Huakaʻi at Hilton Vacation Club Kāʻanapali Beach is the best all-round oceanfront luau. The Royal Lahaina's Myths of Maui is the longest-running.

**Does the Hilton have a luau on Maui?**
Yes. Huakaʻi Lūʻau is hosted at Hilton Vacation Club Kāʻanapali Beach, and the Grand Wailea, a Waldorf Astoria (Hilton) resort, hosts The Lūʻau at Grand Wailea.

**Does the Four Seasons Maui have its own luau?**
No. The Four Seasons Resort Maui at Wailea books guests into a nearby Family Luau about 5 minutes away rather than hosting one on its grounds.

**Is the Royal Lahaina Luau in Lahaina?**
No. The Royal Lahaina Resort, home of Myths of Maui, is at the north end of Kāʻanapali Beach, about 10 minutes' drive from Lahaina town.
