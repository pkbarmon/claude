# Is Feast at Lele Open? Lahaina Luaus in 2026 and the Best Alternatives

> **Quick answer:** No. **The Feast at Lele is closed.** Its oceanfront venue at 505 Front Street was destroyed in the August 8, 2023 Lahaina wildfire, and as of September 2026 no reopening date has been announced. Its sister show, **Old Lahaina Luau** (1251 Front Street), survived and **reopened in March 2024**. It's the one luau operating in Lahaina town today.

*Updated September 2026 · Reviewed by the Maui Luau Company editorial team*

The Feast at Lele was one of Maui's most-loved evenings: a small, oceanfront dinner show on Front Street that served a **plated, multi-course meal** matched to four Polynesian cultures, Hawaiʻi, Aotearoa (New Zealand), Tahiti and Samoa, with each course followed by that island's dance. People still search for it every day. This page explains its status and where to find a similar evening now.

## Is Old Lahaina Luau open?

**Yes.** Old Lahaina Luau sits at the north end of Front Street, outside the main burn area. It was among the first major cultural attractions to reopen, in **March 2024**. It runs nightly and often books up **weeks to months ahead** in 2026. Reserve before you arrive on Maui.

What sets it apart:
- **The most traditional program of any major Maui luau:** hula kahiko (ancient) and hula ʻauana (modern) telling Hawaiʻi's story, with Hawaiian-language chant.
- **No Samoan fire knife.** Old Lahaina keeps the show focused on Hawaiian culture.
- A lei greeting, open bar, and a dinner that current 2026 guest reports describe as **family-style, served at the table**.
- Price: 2026 reports put adult tickets at around **$230**. Check the live rate.

Full details: [Old Lahaina Luau guide](/luau-companies/old-lahaina-luau).

## Visiting Lahaina for a luau: what to know

- **Be a respectful visitor.** Lahaina is still rebuilding, and many residents lost homes. Stick to open businesses and public areas, don't enter or photograph fenced burn-zone lots, and follow posted signs.
- **Spending locally helps.** Local leaders have encouraged visitors to support reopened Lahaina and West Maui businesses rather than avoid the area.
- **Parking and traffic.** Allow extra time on the Honoapiʻilani Highway, especially around sunset. See [parking and drive times](/blog/maui-luau-parking-and-drive-times).
- **Staying elsewhere?** The five Kāʻanapali resort luaus are 10–15 minutes north. See [Kāʻanapali luaus compared](/blog/kaanapali-luaus-compared).

## What was on the Feast at Lele menu?

Feast at Lele was known for **pairing each course with an island**. A Hawaiian course, typically kalua pork and fish, came with hula. An Aotearoa (Māori) course came with haka and poi-ball dances. A Tahitian course came with ʻōteʻa drumming. A Samoan course ended with the fire knife. Menus changed over the years, and it was never an all-you-can-eat buffet. For a similar plated approach today, see the Feast at Mōkapu or Te Au Moana above, or our [Maui luau food guide](/blog/maui-luau-food-guide).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the Feast at Lele open in 2026?**
No. The Feast at Lele has been closed since its Front Street venue was destroyed in the August 2023 Lahaina wildfire, and no reopening date has been announced as of September 2026.

**Is Old Lahaina Luau still open?**
Yes. Old Lahaina Luau at 1251 Front Street survived the 2023 fire and reopened in March 2024. It runs nightly and often sells out weeks ahead.

**What is the best alternative to Feast at Lele?**
The Feast at Mōkapu at Andaz Maui in Wailea is the closest match: a small, chef-driven luau with a plated, family-style dinner on the beach. For Hawaiian cultural depth in Lahaina itself, book Old Lahaina Luau.

**Are there any luaus in Lahaina right now?**
Old Lahaina Luau is the one major luau operating in Lahaina town in 2026. Five more resort luaus run 10–15 minutes north in Kāʻanapali, including Myths of Maui at the Royal Lahaina Resort.

**Is the Royal Lahaina Luau in Lahaina town?**
No. The Royal Lahaina Resort, home of Myths of Maui, is in Kāʻanapali, about 10 minutes' drive north of Lahaina town. It wasn't in the fire zone.
