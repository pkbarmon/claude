# Wailea Luaus Compared: Which South Maui Luau Is Worth It?

> **Quick answer:** Wailea has four luaus in 2026. **The Lūʻau at Grand Wailea** ($300–$360) is the award winner and the most expensive once the 24% service charge is added. **Te Au Moana** at the Wailea Beach Marriott (about $225–$325) is the best-value resort luau with a plated dinner. **The Feast at Mōkapu** at Andaz Maui (about $296) is the smallest and most chef-driven. **Aloha Wailea Luau** (from about $160, Wednesdays only) is the budget pick.

*Updated September 2026 · Reviewed by the Maui Luau Company editorial team*

Wailea luaus differ from Ka'anapali luaus in one important way: most serve a **plated or family-style dinner at your own table** instead of a buffet line. That makes the evening calmer and more like a dinner out, and it is why prices run $50–$100 higher than on the west side. This guide compares all four so you can see what the extra money buys.

## The Lūʻau at Grand Wailea: best for a once-in-a-lifetime night

The Grand Wailea's luau was named to **USA Today 10Best's Best Lūʻau in Hawaiʻi list for 2025**. It runs about **2 hours 45 minutes** on the resort's oceanfront lawn facing Wailea Beach. You sit at a private table, eat a three-course family-style feast, drink from a hosted open bar, and take part in an interactive hula lesson before an imu ceremony and a show that ends in fire.

- **Standard vs Aliʻi Premium:** Aliʻi adds front-row reserved tables, a welcome cocktail, premium spirits, and priority imu viewing. For the full breakdown, see [Grand Wailea premium vs standard seating](/blog/grand-wailea-luau-premium-vs-standard-seating).
- **Watch the fees:** The 24% service charge is a resort fee, not a tip. Read our [tipping guide](/blog/luau-etiquette-and-tipping-guide) before adding more.
- **Choose it if:** the budget is set and this is *the* night of the trip.

## Te Au Moana (Wailea Beach Marriott): best value plated luau

*Te Au Moana* means "the ocean tide." It runs on the Marriott's oceanfront lawn looking across the ʻAlalākeiki Channel toward Kahoʻolawe. You get a fresh flower lei, pre-show music, a **three-course dinner served at the table**, an open bar, and a revue covering Hawaiʻi, Tahiti, Fiji and Samoa that ends with a fire knife dance.

- **Pricing:** about $286 standard and $325 premium per adult during the main season. A promotional rate of about $225 is listed for August 16, 2026 through January 31, 2027. Children 6–12 are around $90, and kids 5 and under are free.
- **Parking:** Self-parking is free with validation. Valet is about $55.
- **Choose it if:** you want the Wailea table-service experience without Grand Wailea's service charge.

*You may also see it as the "Honuaʻula Luau" or the "Wailea Marriott luau." These all refer to the same event.*

## The Feast at Mōkapu (Andaz Maui): best for food lovers

The Feast at Mōkapu is the smallest Wailea luau, with only a handful of group tables on the sand at Mōkapu Beach. Executive Chef Isaac Bancaco designs a **three-course ʻohana-style (family-style) menu**, paired with handcrafted cocktails and a cast inspired by the legends of Maui.

- **Price:** about $296 per adult
- **Choose it if:** the food matters as much as the show, and you want to avoid a 400-seat crowd.
- **Skip it if:** you want the loud fire-knife spectacle of the big revues.

## Aloha Wailea Luau: best budget option in South Maui

Aloha Wailea runs **Wednesday evenings only** at Mulligans on the Blue, above the Wailea golf courses. Doors open at 5:30 PM for an artisan market and pre-show activities (lei-making, ʻukulele, Hawaiian games). A Hawaiian feast and open bar follow, then an hour-long hula and fire-knife show.

- **Price:** about $160 general admission, $209 VIP. Kids 6–12 are about $100, and kids 5 and under are free.
- **Choose it if:** you're staying in Wailea or Kīhei and want a luau for about half the resort price.

## Does the Four Seasons Maui have a luau?

The Four Seasons Resort Maui at Wailea doesn't stage its own luau on the property. Its concierge books guests into a **"Family Luau"** about 5 minutes' drive away, with communal tables and a coursed family-style menu. Kids 5 and under are free. The description ("inspired by the ocean tide") matches Te Au Moana, so ask the concierge which luau it is and compare the concierge rate with booking direct.

## What about the Fairmont Kea Lani and Hotel Wailea?

Neither runs a public luau in 2026. Their guests usually go to Te Au Moana or the Grand Wailea, both a few minutes' drive or shuttle away.

## Wailea vs Ka'anapali: should you drive?

If you're staying in Wailea or Kīhei, Ka'anapali luaus are 60–75 minutes away each way, so driving back in the dark after an open bar is a real drawback. A Wailea luau usually wins on logistics even at a higher price. For a lower-cost option nearby, the **[Sugar Beach Sunset Luau in Kīhei](/activities/sugar-beach-sunset-luau-kihei)** costs $100 for an oceanfront sunset show with food and drinks bought on-site. Compare the west side in our [Ka'anapali luaus comparison](/blog/kaanapali-luaus-compared).

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best luau in Wailea?**
The Lūʻau at Grand Wailea is the best-known Wailea luau and was named to USA Today 10Best's Best Lūʻau in Hawaiʻi list for 2025. For value, Te Au Moana at the Wailea Beach Marriott offers a similar plated dinner and open bar for less and has no 24% service charge.

**How much is the Grand Wailea luau with the service charge?**
Standard seating is $300 per adult and Aliʻi Premium is $360, before a 24% service charge and 4.712% Hawaii tax. That comes to about $390 per adult for Standard and about $467 for Aliʻi Premium.

**Is the Wailea Marriott luau the same as Te Au Moana?**
Yes. Te Au Moana is the luau at the Wailea Beach Resort – Marriott. It's also searched as the Wailea Marriott luau and the Honuaʻula luau.

**Does Andaz Maui have a luau?**
Yes. The Andaz Maui luau is called The Feast at Mōkapu. It's a small, chef-driven luau on Mōkapu Beach with a three-course family-style dinner at about $296 per adult.

**What is the cheapest luau in Wailea?**
Aloha Wailea Luau, on Wednesdays at Mulligans on the Blue, starts around $160 per adult with dinner and an open bar. The nearby Sugar Beach Sunset Luau in Kīhei costs $100, with food and drinks sold separately.

**Are Wailea luaus buffets?**
Mostly no. Grand Wailea, Te Au Moana, and the Feast at Mōkapu all serve the meal at your table, either plated or family-style. That is the main difference from the buffet-style luaus in Ka'anapali.
